About Aurora

A hotel built around simple, thoughtful hospitality

Aurora Hotel is a fictional city hotel concept focused on cleanliness, comfort, clear information and practical guest service.

Our story

Designed for modern city travellers

The project imagines a hotel where guests can quickly understand room choices, hotel facilities, arrival information and contact details.

The design avoids unnecessary complexity and gives priority to clear navigation, readable content and responsive layouts.

Guest journey

A simple four-step stay

  1. Choose. Compare room categories and key amenities.
  2. Plan. Use the demonstration booking form to review your stay details.
  3. Arrive. Check in from 14:00 and contact reception if you need help.
  4. Stay. Use hotel facilities and check out by 12:00.
